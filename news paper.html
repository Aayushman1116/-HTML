MAKOTO SHIINKAI

The Person Behind Everything:

Makoto Niitsu (新津 誠, Niitsu Makoto; born February 9, 1973), known as Makoto Shinkai (新海 誠, Shinkai Makoto), is a Japanese filmmaker and novelist. Shinkai began his career as a video game animator with Nihon Falcom in 1996, and gained recognition as a filmmaker with the release of the original video animation (OVA) She and Her Cat (1999). Shinkai then released the science fiction OVA Voices of a Distant Star in 2002 as his first feature with CoMix Wave, followed by his debut feature film The Place Promised in Our Early Days (2004). and he is considered to be one of Japan's most commercially successful filmmakers.His three most recent films Your Name (2016), Weathering with You (2019), and Suzume (2022), collectively known as "Disaster trilogy" , are all among the highest-grossing Japanese films of all time , both in Japan and worldwide at the time of their release.

Interview

Early life

Makoto Shinkai was born in Koumi, Nagano. His family runs a construction company. Shinkai studied Japanese literature at Chuo University, where he was a member of the juvenile literature club and drew picture books. Shinkai traces his passion for creation to the manga, anime and novels he was exposed to in middle school.

CAREER

  • Indie Origins and the Falcom Era (1996–2002)

    • Shinkai began his career producing video clips and opening animations for titles like Ys at the game company Nihon Falcom. During his off-hours, he developed independent projects. In 1999, he released the five-minute black-and-white short She and Her Cat. His major breakthrough arrived in 2002 with the 25-minute OVA Voices of a Distant Star. Shinkai created almost the entire sci-fi short single-handedly on his Power Mac G4, handling the directing, writing, animating, and initial voice acting.

  • Developing a Signature Aesthetic (2004–2013)

    • Partnering with CoMix Wave Films, Shinkai transitioned to feature-length projects. His 2004 debut, The Place Promised in Our Early Days, was followed by 5 Centimeters per Second (2007). This episodic drama solidified his reputation for telling melancholic stories about emotional and physical distance. While he briefly experimented with traditional fantasy in Children Who Chase Lost Voices (2011), he returned to modern, rain-slicked Tokyo with the visually dense medium-length film The Garden of Words (2013).

  • Blockbuster Success and the Disaster Trilogy (2016–Present)

    • In 2016, Shinkai released Your Name (2016). The film shattered international box office records and demonstrated his ability to blend emotional intimacy with broad, crowd-pleasing fantasy elements.

Following this success, he directed an unofficial "disaster trilogy":

  • Disaster Trilogy
    1. Weathering with You (2019)
    2. Suzume (2022)
They explore catastrophic events—meteors, endless rain, and earthquakes—reflecting Japan's real-world anxieties following the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake.
